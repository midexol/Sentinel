Connect a wallet to begin watching.

Sentinel monitors the wallet you connect for stalled transactions on Base, diagnoses why, and resolves them inside limits you control.

  • i. Read-only until you add a signing key in Settings : it can watch and diagnose without ever touching funds.
  • ii. Dry-run mode lets you see real detection and diagnosis with no broadcast at all.
  • iii. Every clamp, fallback and resolution is written to the ledger : nothing acts silently.
